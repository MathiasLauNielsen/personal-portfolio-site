# Offers

## What is sold

- **Data platform expertise:** pipelines, warehouse, data models and reporting; built from scratch, fixed when slow, costly or fragile, or a senior engineer embedded in the team. Since 2026-09-30 the page also names machine learning in production and forecasting: models that predict revenue, incoming calls, membership movements, churn, unemployment and which records are worth processing, all put into production by Mathias. Decided the same evening: machine learning is a named strength inside this offer, not a third offer ([source](../raw/2026-09-30-ml-models.md)).
- **AI coding expertise:** coding agents set up properly in a codebase, with conventions, guardrails, connections and hands-on training.

## Ways to buy

- **Hours:** an embedded senior engineer, part-time or full-time, at an hourly rate agreed up front. The most common way.
- **Fixed-scope products at a fixed price:**
  - **Data platform review:** a written, prioritised report on cost, fragility and what to fix first, plus a walkthrough.
  - **AI coding setup:** agents set up in one team or codebase, with training.

Prices and rates are never written here (the repo is public, see [schema](../schema.md)).

## Durations (estimates, 2026-09-30)

Shown on the site as working days and calendar time. These are Claude's estimates, not measured; replace them after the first engagements.

| Product | Work | Calendar | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data platform review | 5–8 days | 2–3 weeks | Interviews ½–1 day, cost analysis 1–2, reliability review 1–2, report 1–2, walkthrough ½. Calendar time is mostly waiting for access and interviews. |
| AI coding setup | 6–10 days | 3–4 weeks | Discovery 1 day, codebase setup 2–3, connections 1–3, training 1, follow-up after two weeks of use 1–2. |

The AI coding setup estimate assumes a follow-up after two weeks of use, which the product text on the site does not mention yet.
