Claude Code session on 2026-10-11, right after the blog review ([review](2026-10-11-blog-review.md)).

## What Mathias said

"And a rule: We can never use my own contractual things here. It's purely theoretical in the blogs"

## What was done

- The "An example" section was removed from all three published posts: the ranking result (72% of the valuable cases with a quarter of the budget), the nightly job (46.2 million rows rewritten to change 683,000) and the revenue forecasting he has built. The posts now go straight from the business part to the theory.
- The two blog figures that drew numbers from client work (`bandits-ranking-result`, `nightly-rewritten-vs-changed`) were deleted, and blog figures can now only be `illustration` or `published` (a redrawing of a published source named in the caption). The "measured" and "tested" labels no longer exist for blog figures.
- `CLAUDE.md` records the rule. Client work is shown on the case pages, not in the blog.
