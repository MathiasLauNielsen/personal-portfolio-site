## Where the new content comes from

Nothing new is claimed about Mathias:

- **Product steps:** the basis of the duration estimates in [Offers](../concepts/offers.md). The review is interviews, cost analysis, reliability review, report and walkthrough. The setup is discovery, codebase setup, connections and training. No day counts are given per step.
- **What the AI coding setup leaves behind:** the closing of the agent case, which already says what he sets up for a team.
- **AI coding FAQ answers:** the agent case (written rules, checks before merge, an automatic safety check that blocks reading credentials, secrets known by name only); "I measure before and after"; and the restructuring at Ase from the [positioning facts](2026-09-30-positioning.md).
- **The data FAQ:** the platforms he has been responsible for and the mentoring at Ase (positioning facts), and the platform-first reasoning for machine learning ([ML decision](2026-09-30-ml-models.md)).

The AI coding FAQ is quick win 8, which was held back on 2026-09-30 because it would state new claims about his methods. It is now written from the published agent case, so each answer can be checked there. Mathias should still read it once, since it speaks in his voice.
