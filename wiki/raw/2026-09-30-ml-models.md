Stated by Mathias in a Claude Code session on 2026-09-30, late evening, after the positioning PR had gone live.

- "How much should ML/data science factor in? For me it's the most fun part." Models he has deployed:
  - Copyright Agent: revenue prediction; the model at the core of selecting what to process.
  - Ase: incoming calls, membership movements, unemployment in society, churn.
- Claude's recommendation, which he let stand: raise machine learning as a named strength inside the data platform offer, not as a third offer. Reasons: a stranger's site sells one or two things; ML projects are the hardest to fix-price and usually need the platform first; his ML evidence is strongest where it is tied to platform ownership, with models measured in production against what they replaced.
- Shipped: a sixth buyer trigger on the data platform page ("You know what you want to predict, and nobody has built it"), the six kinds of models named under "Machine learning in production", "a first prediction model" as a project type, and the Ase models on the About page.
- He also asked for an SEO pass ("if no one sees it, it doesn't matter") and whether Claude can update his LinkedIn. Answer: no LinkedIn connection exists in this setup; Claude can draft the text.
